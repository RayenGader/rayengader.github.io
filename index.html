I do both, and that is the whole point of how I work: I commission and tune the detection platforms across a client estate — writing the use cases, authoring the correlation rules, cutting the false positives — and then I am the L2 analyst who investigates what those same rules escalate. Profiling APT34 (OilRig)

MITRE ATT&CK G0049 · threat intelligence

A full profile of the Iranian espionage group tracked as APT34 / OilRig: tradecraft mapped across the kill chain, three malware families taken apart, and the command-and-control infrastructure characterised.

Intelligence is only worth the detection it produces, so the deliverable was not the report — it was a set of actionable detection rules and the matching MITRE D3FEND countermeasures. The group's signature is DNS: both POWRUNER and BONDUPDATER tunnel their C2 over DNS queries, which is exactly where the detection effort pays.

T1071.004 · DNS tunnelling C2
The signature of this group. POWRUNER and BONDUPDATER both carry tasking and output inside DNS queries, because DNS leaves nearly every network unfiltered. Detection lives in the shape of the traffic, not the payload: query volume per domain, subdomain entropy, record types that a normal client never asks for. D3FEND countermeasures mapped: DNS traffic analysis · network traffic filtering · executable allowlisting · credential hardening.
